Dryer Chamber Temperature Monitor

✅ Pre-Wired Notice: All internal control components (microcontroller, 5V power supply, MAX485 communication module, 20x4 LCD, LED indicators, and alarm relays) are already completely wired and tested. You only need to connect the 5 external connections below.
1
Mechanical Enclosure Mounting

The system is housed inside an industrial weatherproof junction box engineered for two-stage installation:

  • Front Cover / Door: Contains the entire circuit assembly, LCD screen, and switches.
  • Back Base: Completely blank with corner mounting holes for easy fastening.
Mounting Procedure:
  1. Loosen the 4 front corner screws and detach the front cover.
  2. Fasten the blank back base firmly to your panel board or wall using mounting screws.
  3. Route sensor, power, alarm, and network cables through the cable glands.
  4. Screw the front cover back onto the base once electrical wiring is completed.
2
Main Power Connection (220V AC)

Two power leads are extended inside the box for direct 220V AC single-phase supply:

RED WIRE

Connect to 220V AC Phase / Line (L)

BLACK WIRE

Connect to 220V AC Neutral (N)

After wiring, switch ON the main rocker switch on the enclosure. The 20x4 LCD and status LEDs will illuminate.

3
Alarm Warning Light (Kosmos Rotating Beacon)

A dedicated 2-pin connector is pre-installed on the TOP of the junction box:

  • Connect the two leads from your Kosmos rotating warning light directly to this top connector.
  • The controller's internal relay will automatically illuminate and rotate the beacon light during over-temperature, under-temperature, or sensor fault conditions.
4
Temperature Sensor Wiring (Multispan MS-1208-M1 RTD)

Connect your 3-Wire PT-100 RTD sensors from the dryer chambers to the terminal barrier on the Multispan meter:

Chamber # Meter Channel (+) Terminal (Comp) Terminal (-) Terminal
Chamber 1 CH-1 Terminal 1 Terminal 2 Terminal 3
Chamber 2 CH-2 Terminal 4 Terminal 5 Terminal 6
Chamber 3 CH-3 Terminal 7 Terminal 8 Terminal 9
Chamber 4 CH-4 Terminal 10 Terminal 11 Terminal 12
Chamber 5 CH-5 Terminal 13 Terminal 14 Terminal 15
Chamber 6 CH-6 Terminal 18 Terminal 19 Terminal 20
Chamber 7 CH-7 Terminal 21 Terminal 22 Terminal 23
Chamber 8 CH-8 Terminal 24 Terminal 25 Terminal 26
💡 3-Wire PT-100 Color Rule: Standard 3-wire RTDs have two wires of matching color (e.g. Red, Red) and one distinct color (e.g. White). Connect the single distinct color to the (+) terminal, and the two matching wires to the remaining two terminals.
⏭️ If Dryer has 6 Chambers: Wire Chambers 1 to 6. On the Multispan meter front keypad, press the SKIP button for Channels 7 & 8. The meter and online dashboard will automatically monitor only the 6 active chambers.
5
Network & Internet Connection

Option A: Ethernet RJ45 Cable (Fastest & Preferred)

Plug an active LAN/Ethernet cable into the RJ45 jack on the unit. The system automatically acquires an IP via DHCP and starts cloud transmissions immediately. Look for ETH OK on the LCD display.

Option B: Wi-Fi Hotspot Provisioning

The unit currently holds Chennai Wi-Fi credentials from testing. When powered on in Guwahati without Chennai Wi-Fi:

  1. Turn on the unit. It will scan and fail 4 times (~35 seconds).
  2. It will automatically launch its own setup hotspot:
    SSID: Dryer-Monitor-Setup  |  Password: 12345678
  3. Connect your mobile phone or laptop to `Dryer-Monitor-Setup`.
  4. Open Chrome or any browser and navigate to: `http://192.168.4.1`
  5. Select your Guwahati plant Wi-Fi network (2.4 GHz), enter the Wi-Fi password, and tap Save & Connect.
  6. The unit restarts, connects to your plant Wi-Fi, and begins live telemetry!
